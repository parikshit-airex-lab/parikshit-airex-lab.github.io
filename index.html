About

I am an M.Tech (Research) student in the Department of Computational and Data Sciences at IISc Bengaluru, working in the AiREX Lab under Prof. Sashikumaar Ganesan. My research is on neural operators: networks that learn to solve whole families of partial differential equations instead of one problem at a time.

Before IISc, I studied Production Engineering at VJTI Mumbai. I like problems where the answer has to be both correct and explainable, and I tend to understand a method by building it from scratch.

My work on AW-FNO was presented at ICCFD13 in Milan. See the publication, the projects, or photos from the events in Highlights.
