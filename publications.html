AW-FNO: An Adaptive Approach for Fluid Flow Super-Resolution via Gated Wavelet-Fourier Learning

Mamta Saini*, Parikshit Mahajan*, Gazania Marine Jyrwa, Diya Nagchaudhury

13th International Conference on Computational Fluid Dynamics (ICCFD13), Milan, Italy, July 6–10, 2026

Proposes the Augmented Wavelet Fourier Neural Operator, which fuses the Fourier and Wavelet Neural Operators through an adaptive gated fusion mechanism. It attains the lowest error of the three operators on the Burgers' and Darcy benchmarks, and 57.40 dB PSNR on 4× super-resolution of isotropic turbulence.

* Equal contribution. Paper ID ICCFD13-2026-311.
