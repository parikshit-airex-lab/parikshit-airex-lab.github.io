Highlights

Moments from taking the research out of the lab: conferences, talks, and the conversations around them.

IISc Google Day 2026

Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, 18 September 2026

IISc Google Day is a campus event hosted by Google to connect with the institute's research community. I was selected to present in Track 1: AI/ML and Trustworthy Systems, and opened the track's roundtable as the first of six presenters, with a short technical pitch titled AW-FNO: A Neural Operator for Parametric Partial Differential Equations, followed by Q&A with Google Research and Engineering leads.

ICCFD13, Milan

Milan, Italy, July 2026

I presented AW-FNO paper at the 13th International Conference on Computational Fluid Dynamics, with travel supported by an IISc GARP grant. The highlight was a conversation with a CFD consultant who had independently arrived at the same idea of fusing Fourier and wavelet neural operators, a good sign that the approach is on the right track.
Parikshit Mahajan, IISc Bengaluru
