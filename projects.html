AW-FNO: Augmented Wavelet Fourier Neural Operator

Dec 2025 – Jul 2026

A neural operator with two branches, one working in Fourier space and one in wavelet space, joined by an adaptive gated fusion mechanism. On Darcy flow, the gate learns on its own to lean on the wavelet branch near the notch, where Fourier methods struggle. Benchmarked on Burgers', Darcy flow, Navier–Stokes, and super-resolution of homogeneous isotropic turbulence.

PyTorch, FNO, WNO, trained on an NVIDIA RTX A6000 GPU
